## Get Started in 4 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Obtain Your API Key">
    Contact Dutchman Bridge to receive your partner API key. You'll use this key to authenticate every request to the Partner API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register Your First Customer">
    Call `POST /api/v1/partner/v1/customers` with the customer's basic details to create a customer record under your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete KYC">
    Walk your customer through the KYC tier process — starting with Tier 1 (BVN/NIN) and progressing to higher tiers as their usage requires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Transactions">
    Once KYC is approved, your customer's wallet is active. Deposit funds, execute swaps, and send money globally using the Partner API.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All Partner API requests go to `https://app.dutchmannetwork.online` and must include your `x-api-key` header. See [Authentication](/authentication) for details.
</Note>
